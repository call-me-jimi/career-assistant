What changed

The attention list is gone. The only thing added to your existing page is six numbers and a link — nothing that behaves like a reminder.

The hub

The table is now the only page that needs to be complete. Landing launches, detail inspects — this is where a job search is actually run.

Menu, not columns

Eleven actions won't fit as columns and shouldn't be a drawer. A menu keeps the row scannable and makes every scenario exactly two clicks.

Silence, softened

No archive page, no bulk write-off, no closed_at. Just a pill that counts days and a filter chip. If you give up, you Withdraw — and that is tracked.

Smooth at any size

Grouping absorbs the pile. Quiet groups collapse to one line, so the page is a constant nine or ten rows whether you have 51 or 200 — no pagination, nothing hidden behind navigation. Collapse state is remembered between visits.

Launched, not re-picked

Starting an assistant from a row carries profile, job and round with it. The numbered chat picker never appears — see step 7.

Thirty is a setting

The quiet line lives in settings.json. It drives one counter, one filter chip and one group heading, so moving it moves all three together.

Still true

Nothing ever leaves. Withdrawal is the only exit and it is yours to choose — the pile stays honest about how a long search actually looks.

Why duplicate

Your data has Simon Kucher twice and Jupus twice. Re-applying to a company you know is a real pattern, and today it means starting from an empty form.

Notes without audio

Most rounds won't be recorded. A plain note on the timeline is the difference between a timeline with gaps and one you can actually read back.

Not covered

Follow-up reminders. You have next_step and next_step_at in the schema and nothing reads them — leaving them unused rather than building reminders you said you didn't want.

What to judge

Settled. A build order, worst risk first

Every open question from the last three revisions is answered and drawn. What remains is sequencing — and the sequence is driven by one fact: the learning loop has 2 entries across 51 applications, so anything that displays learnings is worthless until capture is cheap.

  • 01

    Capture — the row menu and the five record-keeping panels

    Rejection with reason, schedule a round, offer, hold, withdraw. Drops feedback.outcome. This is the only step that changes whether the product works, because it is the only one that produces data.

  • 02

    The table — grouping, Waiting, remembered collapse

    Status column out, group headings in. derive_status() starts reading the clock for the quiet line, which needs an injectable now and a reworded invariant in CLAUDE.md. Threshold to settings.json.

  • 03

    Pre-seeded launches

    journey_id, profile_id, interview_id on POST /api/sessions, and let _continue_phase() pick the landing node it already knows. Small change, removes the worst duplication in the app.

  • 04

    The job page — one timeline

    Merges the drawer and the detail page, fixes the created_at / scheduled_at contradiction, adds hold, withdraw, delete, the CV in the header, and duplicate-carrying-feedback.

  • 05

    Learned — profile scoping and the shared pool

    One boolean per playbook item, prompts read own-plus-shared, promotion offers a costed re-run, sharing is one-way. Last because by then there will be something to show.

The honest risk: steps 02–05 are pleasant to build and step 01 is the one that matters. If capture doesn't get easier than the spreadsheet, the rest is a nicer shell around the same empty playbook.
